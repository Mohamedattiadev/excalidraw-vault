## Topics

| Topic                              | Items                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Matrix operations                  | Eigenvectors and eigenvalues, determinant for 3x3 matrix, Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) |

| Interpolation I and II             | Polynomial interpolation, 3-point and 4-point examples                            |

| MLE                                | Likelihood, Gaussian distribution, Poisson distribution, full process under the hood |
